When and how should I take Newt?

Take one stick pack in the morning or before any task requiring focused effort — mix into 12–16 oz of cold water, stir, and drink. Most users feel effects within 20–30 minutes and stay sharp for 5–7 hours. Avoid taking it within 6 hours of bedtime due to the caffeine content.

How much water do I mix it with?

12–16 oz (350–475 ml) is the sweet spot — enough to fully dissolve the powder and keep the lemon lime flavor refreshing, not overpowering. More water makes it lighter; less makes it more concentrated. Avoid very hot liquids, as heat can degrade some of the nootropic compounds.

What's in Newt — and why does it work?

Each stick contains Caffeine Anhydrous (200mg) for fast energy, L-Tyrosine (800mg) to sustain mental drive under stress, L-Theanine (150mg) to smooth caffeine's edge and eliminate jitters, Alpha GPC (600mg) — the brain's preferred choline source for memory and focus — plus electrolytes (400mg sodium) and Acacia Fiber for gut support. Every ingredient is at its clinically studied dose. No fillers, no proprietary blends.

Why is Newt better than coffee or energy drinks?

Coffee gives you caffeine and a crash — Newt pairs caffeine with L-Theanine (the combination most studied for calm, jitter-free alertness) and adds Alpha GPC and L-Tyrosine that coffee simply doesn't have. The result is 5–7 hours of smooth, focused energy versus the 2–4 hour spike-and-crash cycle most people accept as normal.

When will I start noticing results?

Most users feel a clear difference on day one — longer focus, no jitters, no afternoon crash. Cognitive compounds like Alpha GPC build up with consistent daily use, so mental clarity and recall tend to improve further after 1–2 weeks. For best results, take it at the same time each morning.
