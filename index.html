When and how should I take Newt?

Take one stick pack in the morning or before any task that demands focused effort. Mix the contents into 12–16 oz of water, stir or shake well, and drink. Most people feel effects within 20–30 minutes. We don't recommend taking it within 6 hours of bedtime due to the caffeine content.

How much water do I mix it with?

12–16 oz (350–475 ml) works best. More water makes it lighter and slightly less sweet; less water makes it more concentrated. The lemon lime flavor is designed to taste refreshing either way — just avoid very hot liquids, as heat can degrade some of the nootropic compounds.

What's in Newt — and why does it work?

Each stick contains Caffeine Anhydrous (200mg) for fast-onset energy, L-Tyrosine (500mg) to sustain dopamine levels under mental stress, L-Theanine (150mg) to smooth caffeine's edge and prevent jitters, Alpha GPC (600mg) — the brain's preferred choline source for memory and focus — and Acacia Fiber (3g) for gut support. Every ingredient is at its clinically effective dose. No fillers, no proprietary blends, no artificial sweeteners.

Why is Newt better than coffee or energy drinks?

Coffee and energy drinks give you caffeine alone — which delivers energy but also jitters, anxiety, and a hard crash. Newt pairs caffeine with L-Theanine (the combination studied most for calm alertness) and adds cognitive compounds like Alpha GPC and L-Tyrosine that coffee simply doesn't have. The result is 5–7 hours of smooth, jitter-free focus versus 2–4 hours of wired-then-crashed from a typical cup of coffee.

When will I start noticing results?

Most users feel a noticeable difference on day one — especially the absence of jitters and the longer, smoother focus window compared to coffee. Cognitive compounds like Alpha GPC can build up over consistent daily use, so many users report sharper mental clarity and better recall after 1–2 weeks of daily use. For best results, take it consistently at the same time each morning.
